Staff Hub Text Message Terms

Enchanted Medical Aesthetics (Ormond Beach, DeLand and Palm Coast, Florida) sends text message alerts to its own employees through the Enchanted Staff Hub app.

Who gets texts

Only current Enchanted Medical Aesthetics staff who sign in to the Staff Hub, enter their mobile number in My settings → Text messages, and tick the box agreeing to receive texts. Texts are never sent to patients or customers, and no one is signed up automatically.

What we send

Work alerts only: urgent and important office announcements, and reminders to read them. Messages include a short title and a link to the post in the Staff Hub. They never include patient information or marketing.

How often

Message frequency varies, typically a few messages a month. Texts are sent only during each person's work hours unless an urgent alert requires otherwise.

Cost

Enchanted Medical Aesthetics does not charge for these messages. Message and data rates from your mobile carrier may apply.

How to stop

Reply STOP to any message to stop all texts, or choose Never under My settings → Text messages in the Staff Hub. Reply HELP for help, or contact the office manager at info@enchantedmedicalaesthetics.com.

Privacy

Your mobile number is visible only to you and is used only to send Staff Hub work alerts. We do not sell or share mobile numbers or text-messaging consent with third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
